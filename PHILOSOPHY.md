# Philosophy — Iggy Research

## Mission
We are building an ecosystem for creating **original thoughts** in a world where AI increasingly
just copies, processes and averages what has already been said.

AI is not here to think for people. It is here to **speed up human thinking**.

## The problem
- AI-generated content is flooding the internet. It is correct, smooth and all alike.
- It is easy to hand your writing to a machine and slowly lose your own voice.
- Copying is cheap. Original thought is becoming rare — and therefore more valuable.

## Our principles
1. **The human is the author.** You write the text. AI is an editor, a sparring partner and a mirror, not a ghostwriter.
2. **AI asks questions, it doesn't hand you answers.** It points out gaps in reasoning, counterarguments and
   non-obvious connections between notes.
3. **Focus over distraction.** The interface disappears; the thought and the text remain.
4. **A second brain.** Notes link to each other and over time form your own knowledge network, from which
   new ideas are born.
5. **Your data belongs to you.** Markdown files on your disk. You choose the model, the server and what you publish.
6. **From thought to publication.** A good thought should reach people: blog, newsletter, RSS — without
   switching tools.

## What success means
Not the number of generated words, but whether the author:
- thinks faster and deeper than without the tool,
- creates things that could not simply be generated,
- shares them and builds a community of readers around them.

## The ecosystem
**Writing** (a text-focused editor) → **Thinking** (AI editor + linked notes) →
**Publishing** (blog, posts) → **Distribution** (newsletter, RSS) → **Conversation** (readers, replies,
new notes), and back to the start.
